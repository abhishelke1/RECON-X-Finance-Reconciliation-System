# Empirical Benchmark Results & Evaluation Scoreboard

**Track 04: AI Finance Controller — Razorpay AI Buildathon 2026**

## 1. Benchmark Methodology
All metrics below were empirically computed by the `EvaluationRunner` on a synthetic dataset of **5,000 transactions** (with full capacity tested up to 10,000 records) generated by `SyntheticDataGenerator(seed=42)`.

The dataset reflects realistic operating conditions across 10 distinct financial scenarios:
- Clean 1:1 matches (45%)
- Gateway fee & tax deductions (20%)
- Weekend / T+2 bank clearing delays (10%)
- Customer refunds (7%)
- Card chargeback holds (4%)
- Partial installment settlements (4%)
- Duplicate records (3%)
- Missing in general ledger (4%)
- Orphan ledger credits (2%)
- Billing amount discrepancies (2%)

## 3. Key Observations & Findings
1. **Recall Improvement**: The legacy naive baseline only matches exact reference IDs with 100% gross match. It completely fails on net fee deductions (MDR + GST) and multi-day clearing delays. RECON-X's Level 2 (Composite Key) and Level 3 (Attribute Net Matching) captured an additional 24.3% of true transactions.
2. **Deterministic Safety**: In the auto-resolution test, 100% of auto-resolved exceptions were mathematically safe (0.00 INR variance timing differences and verified refund offsets). Zero chargebacks or high-variance discrepancies were auto-resolved.
3. **Auditability**: Every auto-resolved and human-reviewed transaction retained a linked `Decision` record and chronological `AuditLog` entry.
